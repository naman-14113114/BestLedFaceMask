# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and other markets comparing at-home LED face masks before purchasing. They need clear country-specific pricing, availability, product links, ratings, and credible expert-led guidance.

## Product Purpose

Best LED Face Mask publishes localized buyer guides, comparison pages, and educational articles. Success means visitors can confidently compare products, understand tradeoffs, and reach the correct regional product page without inconsistent identity, imagery, ratings, or market information.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, and practical. The site should feel like a careful buyer guide with consistent evidence, restrained promotional emphasis, and approachable expert authority.

## Anti-references

Avoid mismatched product imagery, fabricated-looking medical identities, yellow marketplace-style rating stars, exaggerated decorative badges, country-link mistakes, generic AI landing-page patterns, and visual changes that conflict with the established green editorial system.

## Design Principles

- Keep expert identity, product facts, and market behavior consistent everywhere.
- Make country context and purchase destination immediately understandable.
- Prefer accurate product-specific visuals over decorative substitutions.
- Use ratings and ranking signals honestly and accessibly.
- Preserve the existing editorial design while improving clarity and trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable responsive typography, useful image alt text, keyboard-accessible links and controls, accurate screen-reader labels for ratings, sufficient color contrast, and reduced reliance on color alone to communicate rank or score.
